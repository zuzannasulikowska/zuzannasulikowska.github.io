WARSAW, PL

OSOWA GÓRA RESIDENCE

AREA 340 m²
YEAR 2026
SCOPE Full Interior & Furniture Design

The Osowa Góra residence explores the juxtaposition between austere modernism and warm tactile naturalism. Built around an open central living hub, the interior allows light to play against hand-troweled lime plaster walls and matte black steel joinery.

Custom furniture pieces were fabricated specifically for this project, including the solid travertine kitchen island and recessed oak cabinetry with brushed bronze handles.
